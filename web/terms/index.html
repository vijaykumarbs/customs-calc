1. What the service is

CustomsCalc provides indicative Indian customs duty and landed-cost calculations for import planning. It is an information tool for businesses and professionals. It is not legal, tax, accounting or customs advice, and it is not a substitute for a licensed customs broker, a Bill of Entry filing or an ICEGATE assessment.

2. Data sources and caveats

  • Rates are derived from the CBIC working tariff as on 30 June 2025, with a curated set of later effective-rate changes and FTA schedules. They are updated manually and may lag a notification.
  • 11,906 lines carry a line-level statutory BCD rate, including 144 compound or specific-rate parses that are approximate. The remaining 2,510 lines are dataset-only and flagged for verification. Five limited PTAs are shown at MFN.
  • Anti-dumping duty, countervailing duty, safeguard duty, tariff-rate quotas, specific duties, valuation adjustments and origin verification are excluded. They can change the amount payable.
  • Every result carries a verification flag. Always confirm the rate and the assessment on ICEGATE before filing a Bill of Entry.
