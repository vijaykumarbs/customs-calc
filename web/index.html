Questions

Good to know

Is this the final duty I will pay?

No. It is an indicative calculation on the CIF assessable value. Anti-dumping duty, safeguard duty, tariff-rate quotas, specific duties, valuation adjustments and origin verification can change the amount. Confirm on ICEGATE before filing a Bill of Entry.

What is the difference between statutory and effective BCD?

The First Schedule sets the statutory rate. Exemption notifications often reduce the rate actually payable. The calculator starts from the effective rate where we have it and shows both numbers in the result.

Do FTA rates apply automatically?

No. You need a valid certificate of origin or self-declaration, direct consignment and compliance with the product-specific rules of origin. The calculator shows the rate you can claim when those conditions are met.

How fresh is the data?

The current extract is built from the CBIC working-tariff chapter PDFs as on 30.06.2025, with a curated set of post-Budget 2026 effective-rate changes. Every result carries a source note and a verification flag.
